Excel recalculates formulas when you open the file. Cached numbers in this page are independent sums from Frequency, not Excel’s engine.

What this does not do

It does not retune Frequency, RTP, or jackpot math. Combined multi-price Delivery packs are not inputs — upload the per-price PPS. If ExcelJS cannot open a file, open it in Excel and Save As .xlsx first. Native Excel formatting will be close to the KY templates, not a pixel-perfect clone of every theme index.
